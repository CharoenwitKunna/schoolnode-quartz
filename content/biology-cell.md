# Cell Biology

The cell is the basic unit of life. All living organisms are made of one or more cells.

## Key organelles
- **Nucleus** — stores DNA and controls the cell.
- **Mitochondria** — release energy via respiration.
- **Ribosomes** — build proteins from amino acids.
- **Cell membrane** — controls what enters and leaves.

Plant cells also have a cell wall, chloroplasts, and a large vacuole for photosynthesis and support.

Energy transfer in cells depends on chemical reactions — see [[chemistry-bonds]] for how atoms bond to form molecules like glucose.
Movement of substances (diffusion, osmosis) follows physical laws covered in [[physics-motion]].
