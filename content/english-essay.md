# Essay Writing Guide

A good school essay has three parts: introduction, body paragraphs, conclusion.

## PEEL paragraphs
- **Point** — make your argument.
- **Evidence** — quote or fact to support it.
- **Explain** — link evidence back to the point.
- **Link** — connect to the next paragraph.

Practise by writing about [[history-ww2]] — argue what the biggest turning point was.
Science write-ups (e.g. a report on [[biology-cell]]) use the same PEEL logic for conclusions.
