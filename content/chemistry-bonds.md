# Chemical Bonds

Atoms join together by chemical bonds to make molecules and compounds.

## Main bond types
- **Ionic** — electrons transferred (e.g. NaCl table salt).
- **Covalent** — electrons shared (e.g. H2O, O2).
- **Metallic** — electrons pooled between metal atoms.

Bond energy decides whether a reaction releases or absorbs heat. Photosynthesis and respiration in [[biology-cell]] are driven by breaking and forming bonds.
