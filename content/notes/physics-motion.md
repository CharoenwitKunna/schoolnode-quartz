# Motion in Physics

Motion describes how an object's position changes over time.

## Key equations (constant acceleration)
- v = u + at
- s = ut + ½at²
- v² = u² + 2as

Speed is distance over time; velocity adds direction. Forces cause acceleration (F = ma).

Diffusion of molecules in [[biology-cell]] follows the same ideas of movement at small scale.
To rearrange these formulas confidently, revise [[math-algebra]].
