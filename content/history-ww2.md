# World War II — Overview

World War II (1939–1945) was a global conflict between the Axis (Germany, Italy, Japan) and the Allies (UK, USSR, USA and others).

## Key points
- Started with Germany's invasion of Poland, 1 Sept 1939.
- Turning points: Stalingrad (1943), Midway (1942), D-Day Normandy landings (6 June 1944).
- Ended in Europe May 1945, in the Pacific September 1945.

The war accelerated physics and technology (radar, rockets, atomic bomb) — see [[physics-motion]] for the motion science behind ballistics.
